Questions

Billing, plainly

Can I start on Entry and move up later?

Yes. Upgrade from the portal at any time; you pay only the pro-rated difference for the rest of the current period, and nothing is re-installed or migrated.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your data and settings are kept for 30 days. Subscribe at any point in that window and nothing changes for your team; otherwise the workspace is archived and then deleted.

Can I pay by bank transfer?

Yes. Choose bank transfer at checkout; PayHere confirms the deposit within one business day and the subscription activates automatically. Card plans renew automatically; bank-transfer plans send a renewal reminder 14 days before expiry.

How are seats counted?

Vortex and Craft Studio are per named user. iMonitor is per monitored PC; dashboard users are unlimited. Add or remove seats any time; changes are pro-rated on the next invoice.

Do you issue invoices?

Every payment produces a tax invoice in USD that you can download from the portal.

Refunds?

Monthly plans can be cancelled any time and stop at the end of the paid month. Annual plans are refundable pro-rata within the first 30 days. See the refund policy.
